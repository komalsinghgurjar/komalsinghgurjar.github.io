Privacy Policy – PDF Reader Pro

Effective date: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

PDF Reader Pro ("the App") by Komal Singh Enterprises development by Komal Singh is a PDF reading application designed to work locally on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

2. Information Collection

PDF Reader Pro does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers.

The App does not include:

  • Advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • Analytics or tracking services.
  • User accounts or registration.
  • Cloud synchronization.
  • Third-party data collection services.

The App does not request internet access permission and is designed to operate offline.

3. Files and Documents

PDF Reader Pro accesses PDF documents that you choose to open through the Android file picker or other supported Android document access mechanisms.

Your documents are processed locally on your device. The App does not upload, transmit, or share your PDF documents with us or any third party. Access to documents is subject to Android's file access and permission mechanisms.

4. Crash Reports and Diagnostic Information

The App does not automatically transmit crash reports or diagnostic information.

If you encounter a problem, you may voluntarily choose to send a crash log or diagnostic information through your own email application.

  • You control whether to send the email.
  • The email may contain technical diagnostic information relevant to the reported issue.
  • Your email address and additional information included in the email are handled by your email provider.
  • We receive the information only if you voluntarily send the email.

Sending a crash report is entirely optional.

5. Internet Connectivity

PDF Reader Pro does not request the Android INTERNET permission and does not establish internet connections for data collection or transmission.

Any email communication initiated by you is handled separately by your chosen email application.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services that collect user information.

Android, your device manufacturer, or other applications installed on your device may operate under their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a server-side database of user information collected through the App.

PDF documents and locally stored application data remain under your control on your device. You can delete documents or uninstall the App using Android's standard functionality.

If you voluntarily email us a crash report, the information received may be retained as necessary to investigate and resolve the reported issue.

8. Children's Privacy

PDF Reader Pro does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The App is designed to minimize privacy risks by not collecting personal information through its functionality.

9. Security

The App processes documents locally on your device and does not transmit them to external servers. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and its stored documents.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published at the same privacy policy URL, with a revised effective date.

11. Contact Information

For privacy-related questions, feedback, or technical support, you may contact us through the application's available support or feedback options. Or you can email komalsin9h@gmail.com
