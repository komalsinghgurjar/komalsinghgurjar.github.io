Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Screen Lock – One Tap, developed and published by Komal Singh Enterprises ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy. Screen Lock – One Tap is designed to perform its core function directly on your device. The app does not collect, upload, or transmit your personal information to us or to our servers.

1. Information Collection and Use

The app does not require an account, registration, name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.

We do not operate analytics, advertising, tracking, or user-profiling services in the app. We do not maintain a backend server or database for collecting information from users.

2. Internet Access

The app does not request the Android Internet permission and does not communicate with our servers or transmit information over the internet. Its normal features operate locally on your device.

3. Device Administrator Permission

The app optionally uses Android's Device Administrator capability, specifically the Force Lock permission, to allow you to lock your device with a single action.

This permission is used only to perform the screen-lock function when you activate it. The app does not use this permission to monitor your activity, access your messages, read your contacts, collect files, or obtain personal information.

You can revoke Device Administrator access through the app's designated control or Android device settings. Once access is revoked, the app can no longer use the administrator-based locking function.

4. Widgets, Shortcuts and Quick Settings Tile

The app may provide a home-screen widget, launcher shortcut, and Quick Settings tile to make device locking more convenient. These features invoke the app's local screen-lock functionality and do not send your information to us.

5. Optional Crash Reports

The app includes crash-reporting functionality to help diagnose technical problems. Crash information is not automatically uploaded or sent to us.

If you choose to share a crash report, the app opens an email application on your device and prepares a report for you to review. You decide whether to send it, which email application to use, and whether to proceed with sending.

A crash report may contain technical diagnostic information such as an error stack trace, app version, Android version, device-related technical details, and information relevant to the crash. The contents depend on the particular crash and diagnostic report.

The email is sent through the email service you choose, not directly through the app. That email provider may process the report under its own privacy policy. We receive information only if you voluntarily send the email to us. We do not receive or retain a report merely because a crash occurred or because the email screen was opened.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

If you voluntarily send us a crash report by email, the information you choose to include is shared with us through your selected email provider for the purpose of investigating the reported technical problem.

7. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a server-side database of user information and therefore do not retain personal data collected through the app. The app's ordinary operation does not send personal information to us.

If you voluntarily email us a crash report, we may retain that email and its diagnostic information only for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate and resolve the reported issue, subject to applicable legal obligations.

You can delete the email from your own email account. To request deletion of a crash report you have sent to us, contact us using the developer contact details available on our Google Play listing.

8. Data Security

Since the app does not transmit user information to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side collection of user data. When you voluntarily send a crash report, its transmission and storage are handled by the email provider you choose and are subject to that provider's security practices.

9. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. It does not require users to provide personal information to use its core functionality.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, privacy practices, or applicable legal requirements change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact Information

For questions, privacy-related requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer, Komal Singh Enterprises, using the developer contact details provided on the app's Google Play Store listing.
