1. Purpose of the App

Screen Rotation is an Android utility application that allows users to select and control their device's screen orientation.

Depending on the selected mode, Android version, device manufacturer, system configuration, and permissions granted, the App may provide options such as:

  • Portrait orientation.
  • Reverse portrait orientation.
  • Landscape orientation.
  • Reverse landscape orientation.
  • Automatic sensor-based orientation.
  • Other supported orientation-control modes.

The availability and behavior of individual modes may vary depending on device and operating system support.

3. Foreground Service and Notifications

Screen Rotation may use an Android foreground service to maintain the selected rotation behavior while you use other applications.

While the service is active, Android may display a persistent notification indicating that rotation control is running.

The notification may provide controls to manage the selected orientation or stop the service. You can also stop rotation control from within the App.

If the service is stopped, interrupted by Android, or terminated by the user, the selected orientation may no longer be maintained.
