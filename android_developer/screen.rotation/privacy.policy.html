Komal Singh Enterprises ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Screen Rotation mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information and describes the permissions it uses.

1. Privacy Overview

Screen Rotation is a local utility application designed to control your Android device's screen orientation. The App does not require an account or registration and does not operate a backend server for collecting user information.

The App does not collect or transmit personal information, location information, contacts, photographs, files, browsing history, or usage analytics to our servers. Your rotation preferences and application settings are stored locally on your device.

The only exception is the optional, user-initiated crash reporting feature described in Section 3.

2. Information Stored Locally

The App may store the following information locally on your Android device to provide its functionality:

  • Your selected screen orientation mode.
  • Application preferences and configuration settings.
  • Other local state required to operate the rotation service.

This information is used only to operate the App. It is not transmitted to us or stored on our servers.

3. Optional Crash Reporting

The App includes an optional crash reporting feature powered by ACRA. If the App encounters an unexpected crash, you may be presented with a crash report dialog.

You can decline the report or choose to send it using your device's available email application. No crash report is automatically transmitted by the App.

If you choose to send a report, the email attachment may contain diagnostic information such as:

  • Crash details and exception information.
  • Stack traces and technical diagnostic information.
  • App and device environment information included in the report.
  • The app version and crash-related details.

The email is addressed to komalsin9h@gmail.com. Your email application and email service provider handle the actual sending of the message.

By choosing to send the report, you authorize the sharing of its contents with us for the purpose of diagnosing crashes, identifying compatibility problems, and improving the App. You may review or edit the email before sending it.

Sending a report is entirely voluntary. If you select the option not to send it, no report is emailed to us through this feature.

4. Permissions and Their Uses

Depending on your Android version and device, Screen Rotation may request special permissions or access to provide its screen rotation functionality.

  • Display over other apps: Used for the app's rotation-control mechanism when required to maintain the selected screen orientation across other applications.
  • Modify system settings: Used as an optional compatibility mechanism to change supported system screen rotation settings.
  • Foreground service: Allows the rotation-control service to continue operating while you use other applications. Android may display a persistent notification while the service is running.
  • Notifications: Used to display the ongoing service notification and provide rotation controls or a way to stop the service.

These permissions are used to provide the functionality requested by the user. They are not used to collect personal information for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information because the App does not collect personal information for these purposes.

The optional crash reporting feature uses your device's installed email application and the email service provider you choose. Once you send a crash report, its handling is also subject to the privacy practices of those providers.

The App does not include advertising networks, third-party analytics services, social media tracking tools, or user profiling services.

6. Data Security

Local application settings are stored within the App's private storage area managed by Android. The App does not maintain a remote database containing user information.

If you voluntarily email a crash report, the security of that email transmission and its storage is also subject to the security practices of your email provider.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored preferences remain on your device until they are changed, cleared, or removed through Android's application data management or uninstallation process.

Crash reports are received only when you voluntarily send them. We use received reports for technical diagnosis and improvement and retain them only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

You may contact us to request deletion of a crash report you previously emailed to us.

8. Children's Privacy

Screen Rotation is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Choices

You remain in control of the App's permissions and functionality. You can:

  • Grant or revoke supported permissions through Android settings.
  • Change or clear locally stored application data.
  • Stop the rotation-control foreground service.
  • Decline any optional crash report.
  • Choose whether to send, edit, or cancel a crash report email.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, functionality, or applicable legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
