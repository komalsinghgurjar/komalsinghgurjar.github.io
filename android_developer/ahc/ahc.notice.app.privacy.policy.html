1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Komal Singh Enterprises ("we", "us", "our", or "the Developer") collects, uses, stores, processes, and protects information when you use the AHC Notice Board mobile application ("App").

AHC Notice Board provides access to court-related notices, announcements, updates, and other information submitted through the application's available features.

We aim to collect and process only information reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the App. This Policy explains our data practices and your available privacy choices.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the features you use, the permissions you grant, and the third-party services integrated into the App.

2.1 Account and Authentication Information

When you register or authenticate, we may process:

  • Email address associated with your account.
  • Firebase Authentication user identifier (UID).
  • Authentication provider and account identifiers.
  • Account creation and relevant authentication timestamps.
  • Account status and associated technical records.

Authentication is provided through Firebase Authentication or other supported authentication infrastructure. We do not intentionally collect or store your Google account password.

2.2 Subscription and Purchase Information

To provide subscription-based features and verify access, we may process:

  • Subscription plan and product identifiers.
  • Subscription status, such as active, expired or cancelled.
  • Subscription expiry and renewal information, where available.
  • Purchase verification identifiers and transaction references.
  • Account identifiers associated with subscription entitlements.

Payments made through Google Play are handled by Google Play and its payment infrastructure. We do not intentionally collect or store complete payment card numbers or payment authentication credentials.

2.3 User-Submitted Content

Depending on available features, users may voluntarily submit:

  • Text posts, notices and announcements.
  • Court names, court-related information and descriptions.
  • Photographs or images of notices and related documents.
  • Other information submitted through application features.

Please do not submit confidential, sensitive, restricted, or third-party personal information unless you have the necessary authority and lawful permission.

2.4 Analytics and Technical Information

We use Firebase Analytics and potentially other technical services to understand application usage and maintain application performance.

Depending on SDK configuration and enabled features, information may include:

  • Application usage and interaction events.
  • Application version and operating system information.
  • Device and technical identifiers.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information.
  • Performance and operational information.
  • Pseudonymous analytics identifiers.

The exact information collected may vary according to the enabled SDKs, device settings, and service configuration.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts.
  • Authenticating users and managing account access.
  • Providing application functionality and services.
  • Publishing and managing submitted notices and content.
  • Verifying subscriptions and managing paid features.
  • Preventing unauthorized access and misuse.
  • Detecting fraud, suspicious activity and security threats.
  • Diagnosing errors and maintaining application reliability.
  • Understanding application usage and improving functionality.
  • Responding to user requests and complaints.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.
  • Protecting and enforcing our legitimate legal rights.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use cloud-based infrastructure and reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information processed through our systems.

Depending on the information, storage may involve:

  • Cloudflare-hosted databases.
  • Cloudflare R2 Object Storage.
  • Firebase and Google infrastructure.
  • Other infrastructure required to operate the App.

Security measures may include:

  • Restricted access to backend systems.
  • Authentication and authorization mechanisms.
  • Secure communication protocols.
  • Access controls and permission checks.
  • Security monitoring and operational safeguards.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security. Although we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed, disclosed, altered, or destroyed through circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

6. Data Retention

We follow a data-minimization approach and generally retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy.

6.1 General Retention Practice

Our usual operational practice is to remove or delete most application-related information within approximately 30 days after it is no longer required for its original purpose.

This is a general retention target and not a guarantee that every category of information will be deleted within exactly 30 days.

6.2 Uploaded Images

User-uploaded photographs and images are hosted using Cloudflare R2 Object Storage.

Images that are removed, no longer required, or scheduled for deletion are generally intended to be deleted within approximately 30 days.

Deletion may take additional time where technical processing, backups, security investigations, or legitimate retention requirements apply.

6.3 Account and Database Records

Account-related information, including email addresses, authentication identifiers, subscription status and associated application records, is generally retained while reasonably necessary to provide the relevant services or fulfil legitimate operational requirements.

When an account deletion request is accepted, we will take appropriate steps to delete associated account information and content, subject to applicable legal retention exceptions.

6.4 Retention Exceptions

We reserve the right to retain specific information beyond the usual 30-day period where reasonably necessary or legally required for purposes including:

  • Compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
  • Legal proceedings, claims, disputes or investigations.
  • Fraud prevention and detection.
  • Security monitoring and incident investigation.
  • Enforcement of our terms, policies and legal rights.
  • Payment, purchase and subscription-related disputes.
  • Necessary financial, accounting or business records.
  • Protection of users, the public or our services.

Information maintained in backups, technical logs, or third-party systems may also remain subject to applicable technical limitations and the respective service providers' retention policies.

Where information is retained beyond the ordinary retention period, we will seek to limit its use to the applicable purpose and delete it when no longer required.
