Request deletion of your account

You can request deletion of your AHC Notice Board account and associated personal information by following the steps below. This page applies to accounts created or used in the AHC Notice Board mobile application.

How to submit a request
  1. Send an email from, or identify, the email address associated with your AHC Notice Board account.
  2. Use the subject line AHC Notice Board – Account Deletion Request.
  3. Tell us that you want your account and associated data deleted. Do not include passwords, OTPs, or payment card details.
  4. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own or are authorized to request deletion of the account.
Email an account deletion request

If the button does not open an email application, send your request manually to komalsin9h@gmail.com.

Data that will be deleted

After we verify and accept a valid request, we will take appropriate steps to delete or de-identify personal information associated with your account from systems under our control, where deletion is permitted. This may include:

  • Your account email address and account profile information.
  • Your Firebase Authentication account and associated user identifier.
  • Account-related records stored in our Cloudflare database infrastructure.
  • Posts or other content associated with your account, where applicable and technically under our control.
  • Images uploaded by you and stored in Cloudflare R2, where applicable and identifiable.
  • Subscription entitlement information, where deletion is permitted and the information is no longer required.

Information that may be retained

We may retain limited information when reasonably necessary or required for legal compliance, accounting, tax obligations, payment or subscription disputes, fraud prevention, security investigations, enforcing our terms, establishing or defending legal claims, or protecting users and our services.

Where information must be retained, we will seek to restrict its use to the applicable purpose and retain it only for the period required or reasonably necessary. Information held in backups, technical logs, or by third-party providers may also remain subject to technical limitations and those providers' own retention policies.

Deletion timeline

Our usual operational practice is to remove or delete most application-related information within approximately 30 days after it is no longer required. This is a general operational target, not a guarantee that every record will be erased within exactly 30 days after a request.

Verification, technical processing, backup cycles, legal obligations, security matters, or other legitimate retention requirements may affect the time needed to complete deletion. We will handle requests within the time required by applicable law.

Subscriptions and Google Play purchases

AHC Notice Board uses services such as Firebase and Google services, Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare database infrastructure, and Cloudflare R2. Information independently processed or retained by these providers is also subject to their applicable privacy, deletion, and retention terms.
