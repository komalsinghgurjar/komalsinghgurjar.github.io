Privacy Policy

Court View AHC is an independent third-party informational and assistive tool. We respect user privacy and aim to collect as little information as possible.

  • This app does not collect, store, share, or sell personal information.
  • This app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or user profiling services.
  • To retrieve court activity information, the app sends standard network requests to the relevant data source.
  • Such requests may contain technical information automatically included by the Android operating system or networking libraries, including IP address, user-agent, and HTTP headers.
  • The developer does not store, or process this information.
  • No user account, registration, login, or identity verification is required to use the app.
  • This app is provided solely as an informational and assistive tool.
  • This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable requirements.
